# Reversi

An Android Othello/Reversi app written in Kotlin. You can play against bots
evolved with [OthelloEngine](https://github.com/HLahoti/OthelloEngine), play
online, and replay your games. Shadow, one of the bots, adapts to you as you
play.

The download page lives in [`docs/`](docs/).
