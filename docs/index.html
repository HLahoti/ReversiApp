Bots

An opponent for every level.

Each bot is a neural network trained by playing itself over hundreds of generations, and there are different bots for different difficulty settings. Pick your colour or leave it to chance.

New · Learns as you play

Shadow gets better every game.

Shadow starts just below Pragg and studies every game you finish against it. Beat it with a line and that line won't work twice: it never loses the same way again, and it repeats what won. Everything it learns stays on your phone, so it grows with you.

History

Every game, kept.

History lists your games against the bots and online: who played which colour, the result and when. Filter by result, opponent or colour, and see where you used a hint or took a move back.

Replays

Watch a finished game again.

Tap a finished game to step through it move by move with the same flips as the real thing, or let it play itself.

Online

Share a code, start a game.

Pick a name, create a room and send the code to a friend. They type it in and you're playing, wherever they are, each with your name on the board. No account to set up.

New · Notifications

Switch apps, don't miss a move.

Get a notification when your friend joins, plays, asks for a rematch or the game ends, and tap it to jump back in. Turn it off any time in Settings.

Learn

New to Reversi? Five steps.

A short walkthrough on a real board covers setup, legal moves, outflanking, flipping and how a game ends. You'll be playing in under a minute.

Pass & play

One phone, two players.

Hand the phone across the table and take turns. Leave in the middle of a game and it's waiting for you when you come back.
